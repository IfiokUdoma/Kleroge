# Kleroge

**A global digital marketplace for real estate.**

Kleroge is a technology platform designed to make real estate discovery, transactions, documentation, and payments more accessible through a unified digital marketplace.

The platform is designed for residential, commercial, industrial, land, and other property categories.

---

## The Problem

Real estate transactions are often fragmented across:

* Brokers
* Property websites
* Agents
* Developers
* Lawyers
* Banks
* Payment providers
* Paper documentation
* Separate verification processes

This creates friction for buyers, sellers, investors, tenants, and property professionals.

Kleroge is being built to bring more of this experience into one digital environment.

---

## The Vision

Kleroge aims to become a global digital infrastructure layer for property transactions.

The long-term vision is not simply another property listing website.

It is a marketplace where users can:

**Discover → Evaluate → Verify → Transact → Document**

property through a unified digital experience.

## Blockchain & Escrow

Kleroge is exploring blockchain as one component of its transaction infrastructure.

The purpose is not to use blockchain simply as a feature.

Instead, blockchain can support areas where transparency, transaction records, digital ownership workflows, or programmable settlement provide practical value.

An escrow-based approach can provide an additional layer of transaction protection by helping separate payment from successful completion of agreed transaction conditions.
